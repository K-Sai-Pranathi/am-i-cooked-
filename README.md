# am-i-cooked-
**Am I Cooked? 🔥** is a fun little website that calculates how “cooked” you are based on your situation—whether it’s an exam tomorrow, too many chapters left, or way too little time. Enter your situation, get your Cooked %, and find out just how doomed (or not doomed) you really are. 😭🔥  Simple, useless, and surprisingly fun.
